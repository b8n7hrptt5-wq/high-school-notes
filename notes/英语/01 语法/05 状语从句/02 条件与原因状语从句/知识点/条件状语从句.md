# 条件状语从句

> [!info] 出自 [[01 条件与原因状语从句]]

## 一句话定义

由 if、unless、as long as、provided that 引导、说明主句动作前提条件的从句。

## 讲解

if 如果：If it rains, we will stay at home.

unless 除非，等于 if...not：You will miss the train unless you hurry.（= If you don’t hurry...）

as long as、provided that 只要：As long as you try, you will improve.
